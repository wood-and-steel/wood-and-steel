# Wood and Steel — Quick Reference

*One per player. Numbers and terms match the [Rulebook](Wood%20and%20Steel%20Rulebook.md).*

## Your turn

1. Take **up to 2 Actions**. Only **Run Trains** may be taken twice.
2. Take **Free Actions** any time in your turn.
3. Press **End Turn**.

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Run Trains | — · each Engine runs once per turn |
| Build Track | Printed price; up to 2 Routes (3 with Employment Office) |
| Buy an Engine | Next level up, less trade-in |
| Buy an Upgrade | $10,000 – $20,000 |
| Acquire an Independent Railroad | $3,000 per Route ($4,000 after any Western delivery) · +1 Railroad Tie |
| Borrow Money | Multiples of $5,000 |
| Repay Debt | $5,000 or $10,000, from $20,000+ Debt, never below $15,000 |
| Discard and Draw a New Contract | — |

**Free Actions:** pick up a Commodity · claim a Market Contract (Mogul or better, 1 at a time) · record Cargo · Undo.

## Track

| | Nobody there | Parallel |
|---|---|---|
| Route | Printed | + $1,000 |
| Mountain Route | Printed | + $2,000 |

Hub: $1,000 less if either City is your Hub or one Route from it.

## Fulfilling a Contract

1. The Engine stops (Timetable: it may go on).
2. Value (× 1⅓ for a Mountain or Challenger) + $3,000 (Upgraded Stations) − Debt Service.
3. Collect the Railroad Ties.
4. Private: choose a new one (2 offers; +1 Hub; +1 Regional Office). Market: you may claim another.

**Debt Service** = $1,000 per $5,000 of Debt. Due: never while you've delivered only in the East · once, on your first Central delivery · on every delivery from your first Western one.

## End of the round

1. Market countdowns tick; expired Contracts are replaced; maybe a new countdown starts at 5.
2. Independent Railroads grow (from the end of round 2): draw them in black.
3. Round Report.

## End of the game

Ends at **30 Railroad Ties from Contracts**. Add:

| Bonus | Railroad Ties |
|---|---|
| Breadth | 1 each for the first 8 Commodities, 2 each for the next 4, 3 each after |
| Depth | 1 + deliveries of your most-delivered Commodity |
| Monopoly | 2 / 3 / 4 (4 / 5 / 6 supplying Cities) |
| Regional Office | 2 |
| Challenger | 2 |
